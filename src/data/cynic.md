Abhimanyu always chooses the harder way because to him the shorter way is done by everyone else.

The older I get, the more I realise how much of life is just pure luck.

Anyone who is privileged therefore owes it to the world to work on the most obnoxiously hard problems, not just build a life for themselves, but find ways to help anyone that they find.

The privileged must risk it all, over and over again, until they are tortured to greatness. 

This is my version of socialism, re-distribute risk. The rich have a moral imperative to embrace more risk.
